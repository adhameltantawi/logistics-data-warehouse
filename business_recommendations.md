## Executive Summary

The fleet has maintained strong and consistent revenue (~$99M/year) with a 100% trip completion rate and 83% fleet utilization over three years. However, four critical areas require leadership attention: **high idle time, below-average on-time delivery rate, fuel cost concentration, and maintenance cost distribution**.

---

## 🔴 Critical Issues — Act Immediately

### 1. On-Time Delivery Rate is Below 50%
- **Finding:** Fleet-wide OTD averages **~44%**, meaning more than half of deliveries are late.
- **Business Impact:** Customer dissatisfaction, potential contract penalties, and risk of losing dedicated accounts ($148M/year).
- **Recommendations:**
  - Audit scheduling windows at the top 10 underperforming facilities.
  - Implement dynamic dispatch with real-time traffic and weather routing.
  - Add a driver incentive tied to weekly OTD performance score.
  - Review load sequencing — late starts cascade into late deliveries across the day.
  - Set a 90-day target: raise OTD to **65%**, then 75% within 6 months.

### 2. Excessive Idle Time — 7 Hours Per Trip Average
- **Finding:** Every trip averages **7.01 hours of idle engine time** (engine on, truck stationary).
- **Business Impact:** At $4+/gallon diesel with ~42 gallons/hour idle burn for a Class 8, this costs an estimated **$2.9M+/year in wasted fuel** across the fleet.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Deploy **idle-reduction technology** (automatic engine shutdown after 5 minutes at idle).
  - Install APUs (Auxiliary Power Units) for sleeper cab comfort without idling.
  - Benchmark facilities causing excessive hold times (detention-heavy locations).
  - Set a fleet idle target of **≤ 3 hours per trip**.

## 🟠 High-Priority Improvements — Address Within 90 Days

### 3. Fuel Cost = 32% of Revenue — Reduce Now
- **Finding:** Total fuel cost is **$95.6M over 3 years** (32% of $298.6M revenue). Fleet average MPG is **6.5**.
- **Industry Benchmark:** Best-in-class Class 8 fleets achieve 7.5–8.5 MPG.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Enforce **speed limit governors** at 62 MPH — each additional MPH above 65 costs ~0.1 MPG.
  - Launch a **driver MPG scorecard** — reward top-quartile fuel performers monthly.
  - Evaluate switching highest-mileage routes to CNG trucks (lower per-mile fuel cost).
  - Optimize fuel card purchasing: consolidate to lowest-cost fuel corridors per route.
  - Projected savings at 7.0 MPG vs 6.5 MPG: **~$7.3M over 3 years**.

### 4. Preventive Maintenance Balance is Off
- **Finding:** Maintenance spend is spread almost equally across 7 types (Engine $890K, Preventive $964K, Repair/Corrective $945K, Brake $912K, Tire $919K, Transmission $880K, Inspection $220K). Corrective repair nearly matches preventive — this is a warning sign.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Increase PM intervals' adherence — corrective-to-preventive cost ratio should be < 0.5; currently it is ~1:1.
  - Prioritize **predictive maintenance** using telematics OBD data (brake wear, tire pressure, engine temps).
  - Negotiate fleet tire contracts — tires are the #2 cost category and highly negotiable at volume.
  - Track **cost per mile** per truck; retire trucks with CPM > fleet average + 20%.

## 🟡 Strategic Opportunities — Plan for 6–12 Months

### 5. Grow the Dedicated Segment — It's Your Profit Engine
- **Finding:** Dedicated bookings generate **$148.1M** (49.6% of revenue) vs. Spot ($75.4M) and Contract ($75.1M) combined at ~50.4%.
- **Why it matters:** Dedicated contracts offer predictable revenue, better asset utilization, and lower per-trip acquisition cost.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Actively upsell top Spot customers to Dedicated agreements — offer 5% rate discount in exchange for volume commitment.
  - Prioritize capacity allocation to Dedicated lanes before Spot.
  - Target a **60/40 Dedicated-to-Spot revenue split** within 18 months.

### 6. Refrigerated Freight is a Hidden Cost Driver
- **Finding:** 50.3% of loads are **Refrigerated** (42,946 loads) vs. 49.7% Dry Van — near equal split.
- **Implication:** Reefer truck operating costs are 15–20% higher (fuel, maintenance, pre-cooling). If Reefer revenue per mile isn't proportionally higher, profitability is eroded.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Calculate **revenue per mile by load type** — if Dry Van yield is equal or higher, shift booking mix toward Dry Van.
  - Introduce a **Reefer fuel surcharge** and cold-chain handling premium.
  - Ensure trailer preventive maintenance prioritizes reefer units — breakdown costs are 3x vs. dry van.

### 7. Top Routes: Double Down on Your Best Lanes
- **Top Revenue Lanes:**

  | Rank | Lane | Revenue Focus |

  | 2 | Seattle → Charlotte | High volume, cross-country |

### 8. Safety Performance: 170 Incidents — DOT Violations are Top Risk
- **Finding:** DOT Violations (39 incidents) and Equipment Damage (35) are the top categories, followed by Accidents (35).
- **Business Impact:** DOT violations trigger audits, CSA score degradation, and potential insurance rate increases.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Launch quarterly **Hours of Service (HOS) compliance training**.
  - Pre-trip inspection compliance rate should be tracked daily — Equipment Damage incidents suggest inspection gaps.
  - Implement a **safety scorecard per driver** — drivers with 2+ incidents in 12 months go through mandatory coaching.
  - Explore dashcam + AI event detection (harsh braking, lane departure, distraction).
  - Target: reduce annual incidents by **30% within 12 months**.
